## Interpretation Notes
- Revenue shows sales contribution; profit shows contribution after modeled costs.
- Compare profit margin alongside revenue; high revenue does not automatically mean high profitability.
- Monthly trends can show patterns, but do not infer causes from synthetic data.
- Treat rankings as practice examples, not real business recommendations.
