# Dataset — Motor Product & Market Intelligence Analytics

## Overview
This folder contains the synthetic dataset used to build the Power BI dashboard.

## Workbook
`motor_product_market_intelligence_dataset.xlsx`

## Tables
- **Products:** Product details, motor types, pricing, and efficiency.
- **Competitors:** Competitor product, pricing, and efficiency information.
- **Market Data:** Market size and market growth information.
- **Sales Performance:** Sales dates, products, regions, units sold, selling prices, and revenue.

## Purpose
The dataset supports product performance analysis, market opportunity assessment, competitor benchmarking, and sales analysis.

**Important:** All data is synthetic and intended for portfolio and learning purposes. It does not represent actual company or market data.
