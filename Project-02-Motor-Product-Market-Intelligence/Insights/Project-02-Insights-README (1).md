# Project 2 --- Motor Product & Market Intelligence Analytics

## Overview

This project analyzes product performance, market attractiveness,
competitor positioning, and sales performance for a synthetic motor
technology company.

The analysis is designed to support product strategy, market
prioritization, competitive benchmarking, and sales planning.

> **Note:** The dataset is synthetic and created for portfolio and
> learning purposes. The insights below demonstrate the type of analysis
> that could be performed with real business data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Product Performance

-   Revenue and units sold can be compared across products and motor
    technologies to identify stronger-performing products.
-   Product-level selling price analysis helps identify differences in
    commercial positioning.
-   Product efficiency provides an additional technical dimension for
    evaluating product performance.

### 2. Market Opportunity

-   Market size analysis highlights larger industries and applications.
-   Market growth analysis helps identify markets with stronger growth
    potential.
-   Comparing market size with growth provides a more balanced view than
    considering either metric alone.

### 3. Competitor Analysis

-   Competitor selling prices can be compared across individual
    competitors and motor technologies.
-   Competitor presence by motor type helps indicate the level of
    competitive activity in each technology segment.
-   Competitor efficiency benchmarking provides a technical comparison
    alongside pricing.

### 4. Sales Performance

-   Revenue and units sold can be evaluated across sales regions to
    identify stronger-performing territories.
-   Application-level revenue and unit analysis helps identify
    applications contributing more strongly to sales.
-   Average selling price by application provides additional context for
    commercial performance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Business Recommendations

### Prioritize High-Potential Markets

Focus detailed sales and marketing evaluation on applications and
industries that combine attractive market size with stronger growth.

### Review Product Priorities

Compare product revenue, units sold, pricing, and technical efficiency
together before deciding which products should receive greater
commercial focus.

### Use Competitor Benchmarking

Regularly compare competitor pricing and efficiency to identify areas
where the company's products may have a potential competitive advantage
or where improvement may be required.

### Strengthen Regional Sales Planning

Use regional revenue and unit performance to identify stronger
territories and investigate opportunities for improving performance in
lower-performing regions.

### Connect Market and Sales Analysis

Market attractiveness should be considered alongside actual sales
performance. A large or fast-growing market does not automatically mean
strong company performance, so both dimensions should be evaluated
before prioritization.

## Dashboard Pages

1.  **Executive Overview** --- Management-level KPIs and overall
    performance.
2.  **Product & Market Analysis** --- Product, motor type, industry,
    application, market size, and growth analysis.
3.  **Competitor Analysis** --- Competitor pricing, presence, and
    efficiency benchmarking.
4.  **Sales & Opportunity Analysis** --- Regional, product, application,
    revenue, units, growth, and selling-price analysis.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Limitations

-   The dataset is synthetic.
-   Market size and market growth values are simulated.
-   Competitor information, pricing, efficiency, and sales values are
    simulated.
-   Real adoption trends and market opportunities would require
    historical business data, validated market research, or other
    reliable external sources.
-   The dashboard should therefore be treated as a portfolio
    demonstration rather than a representation of actual market
    conditions.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how Business Analysis and Power BI can be used
to combine product, market, competitor, and sales information into a
structured decision-support solution.

The analysis provides a framework for identifying attractive markets,
evaluating product performance, benchmarking competitors, and supporting
sales and marketing prioritization.
